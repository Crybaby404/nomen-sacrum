# Initial public source shelf

This is a selective starting bibliography. A bibliography item **does not automatically validate every index row**. Individual records need their own exact passages and editions.

- [ORACC An/Anu](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/amgg/listofdeities/an/) — Mesopotamian deity and writing conventions.
- [ORACC Anunna](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/amgg/listofdeities/anunna/index.html) — gods' group designation.
- [ORACC Namma](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/amgg/listofdeities/Namma/index.html) — Mesopotamian primeval goddess.
- [Takana–Castellano dictionary (2011)](https://www.unicef.org/lac/media/33511/file/diccionario-takana.pdf) — anu and anu yawa.
- [Tacana vocabulary (1902)](https://archive.org/stream/tacanaartevocab00unkngoog/tacanaartevocab00unkngoog_djvu.txt) — historical grandmother/earth terms.
- [Lessons in Hopi (1978)](https://archive.org/details/lessonsinhopi0000kale) — aanu and phonology.
- [Furst on Tacana Anu Eaua (1972)](https://www.samorini.it/doc1/alt_aut/ek/furst.html) — secondary discussion of Hissink & Hahn.
- [Popol Wuj / Christenson](https://www.mesoweb.com/publications/Christenson/PV-Literal.pdf) — K’iche’ ancestral figures.
- [Nahuatl Dictionary](https://nahuatl.wired-humanities.org/node/177401) — tonantzin.
- [Metropolitan Museum of Art: Mut](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/553454) — mother and goddess.
- [Dead Sea Scrolls 4Q120](https://www.deadseascrolls.org.il/explore-the-archive/manuscript/4Q120-1?locale=en_US) — Greek divine name witness.
- [Mishnah Sotah 7:6](https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sotah.7.6) — situational divine-name pronunciation.
- [Kinbank publication (2023)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10208500/) — intended baseline, not yet counted.
- [AIATSIS Code of Ethics](https://aiatsis.gov.au/research/ethical-research/code-ethics) — Indigenous research care.

Links are leads for source verification. No claim of exhaustive coverage or full independent review.
