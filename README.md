# NOMEN SACRUM
### The Sacred Name Atlas · SiMULACRA Studios / Research

**Status:** Working corpus / preliminary index  
**Release track:** v0.1.0-draft · 2026-11-23

*A living comparative research archive of sacred names, forbidden or restricted names, kinship-derived divine titles, and ancient phonetic traditions.*

> **Research disclosure:** This is a collection-first, exploratory corpus. It is geographically and linguistically unbalanced; nonmatching controls are sparse. Pronunciations, earliest attestations, and primary-source links remain incomplete for many entries. **Kinbank spelling-based counts have been supplied and archived, but independently reproduced IPA counts remain unavailable.** A similarity in modern spelling or sound is not evidence of cognacy, transmission, or a universal origin. Nothing here is presented as peer reviewed.

## Project structure
Every full record should separately track: ID; original spelling/script; language and family; dated textual witness; attested lexical meaning; sacred/narrative function; IPA plus published/editorial confidence; status of etymology; whether speaking, writing, knowing, or transmitting the name was restricted; primary source; and outstanding gaps.

**Gather first, test at the end.** Known cognates and borrowings must be grouped. Ordinary terms, ethnonyms, and nonmatching sacred names remain in the comparison set. The project does not infer meanings from arbitrary substrings.

## Cultural care
Only publicly shareable Indigenous-language material belongs in this repository. Do not solicit, reconstruct, or reproduce nonpublic names or rituals. Respect specific communities' knowledge protocols and the [AIATSIS Code of Ethics](https://aiatsis.gov.au/research/ethical-research/code-ethics).
