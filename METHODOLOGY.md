# Research method — Nomen Sacrum

## Design
Collection-first, hypothesis-testing later. The unit is a **source-backed name or lexical record**, not a suspected global root. The initial Atlas is a convenience index, **not an audited linguistic dataset**.

## Uniform schema
For each item record: stable ID; original writing system and transliteration; language, linguistic family and variety; geography; the earliest *verified textual witness* and its date; ordinary lexical meaning (if any); religious role (separately); best-supported IPA with source and confidence; public/restricted/narratively concealed status and the specific restriction; morphology (only if supported in-language); relationship group for known cognates/borrowings; etymological status (established/proposed/disputed/unknown); primary citation; image or textual-reuse permission; gaps.

## Source hierarchy
Original inscriptions and manuscripts; critical editions; historical and community-produced dictionaries; peer-reviewed scholarship; carefully attributed ethnographic reports; clearly marked secondary leads. Never treat the date of a written witness as the origin date of an oral tradition.

## Phonetic method
Normalize to IPA before claiming exact matches. Distinguish vowel length, nasality, tone, consonant place, and stress. English spelling is not IPA. Retrieval labels AN, ANU, AMA, YAM etc. are query aids, **not morphemes**. Do not segment a name unless morphology justifies it.

## Independent observations
Group inherited cognates, equivalent names of the same god, and known borrowings. Related forms can each have a record but do not count as independent global confirmations.

## Controls and tests
Retain ordinary words, names outside the targeted sound families, and negative sacred-name examples. Preregister which sequences, distances, semantic categories, sampling rules, and null tests will be used before measuring an excess over chance. Record language/family and distinct-token denominators. Kinbank counts for /an/, /ana/, /anu/, /nana/ and related targets are **pending**, not zero.

## Access and ethics
DOC: a public witness exists, but historical speech permission is not established. COL: public colonial witness; Indigenous access rules not inferred. NAR: concealment is part of a narrative. VAR: ritual restrictions vary by context. None of these labels authorizes publication of nonpublic knowledge. Follow community restrictions and the AIATSIS Code of Ethics.

## Corrections
Each revision should document the changed field, original source, replacement source, review date, and reason. Publish versioned changes; do not silently retroactively rewrite claimed results.
