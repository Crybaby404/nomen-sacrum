# Contributing

Contributions should be evidence-led. Supply a stable name, original form, language/variety, source citation and exact section/page/tablet when possible, pronunciation evidence, documented meaning, and a clear uncertainty note.

Use published IPA only when the published source actually supplies it; otherwise label an editorial approximation. Preserve tonal, vowel-length and consonant contrasts. Do not infer unattested etymologies from matching romanized syllables.

Respect source copyright and Indigenous cultural protocols. Only publicly shareable Indigenous materials may be entered. Never solicit or publish secret, restricted, ceremonial, or otherwise nonpublic information to complete a record. Follow the [AIATSIS Code of Ethics](https://aiatsis.gov.au/research/ethical-research/code-ethics).

Corrections should identify the previous claim, replacement, reason, and source. Keep false starts visible in the changelog. Do not confuse a familiar mythology with an etymological derivation.
